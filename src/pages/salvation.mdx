Every person eventually asks questions like these:

- What happens after I die?
- Can I know that I will spend eternity with God?

The Bible says we can know.

In 1 John 5:13, Scripture says, "These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life..."

God does not want us to live in uncertainty. He wants us to know the way of salvation.

## The Problem

### Sin separates us from God.

Before someone can be saved, they must first admit they are lost in sin.

Romans 3:23 says, "For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God." Since Adam and Eve, sin has affected every person. Sin is anything contrary to God's holiness, and our sin separates us from Him.

Romans 6:23 says, "For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord."

The penalty for sin is spiritual death and eternal separation from God.

### Good works and religion cannot save us.

People often try to reach God through religion, effort, or morality. But none of those can remove sin.

Proverbs 14:12 says, "There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death."

Ephesians 2:8-9 says, "For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast."

Salvation is not something we earn. It is a gift God gives.

## What Has God Done?

### Jesus Christ paid the price for our sin.

Though we were separated from God, He loved us.

John 3:16 says, "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life."

Jesus died on the cross for our sins and rose again three days later. Through His death and resurrection, He paid the debt we could never pay ourselves.

Romans 5:8 says, "But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us."

## How Can I Be Saved?

### Trust Jesus Christ alone.

To be saved, we must stop trusting ourselves, our works, or our religion, and place our faith in Jesus Christ alone for forgiveness and eternal life.

Romans 10:13 says, "For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved."

If you are ready to turn to Christ, you can call on Him in faith and ask Him to save you.

You may pray something like this:

> Dear God, I know that I am separated from you because of sin. I confess that in my sin, I cannot save myself. Right now, I turn to you alone to be my Saviour. I ask you to save me from the penalty of my sin, and I trust you to provide eternal life to me.
> —Amen
